About

Automotive · Costing

Vehicle and Inventory Costing

Problem

In a dealership, the cost recorded against each vehicle drives both the margin reported on every sale and the value of stock on the balance sheet. Errors show up as wrong margins and misstated inventory.

My Approach

  • Handle vehicle and inventory costing for the group's automotive companies
  • Inventory balance sheet schedules prepared at each month-end and year-end close
  • Margin analysis on vehicle sales for management reporting

Tools

Microsoft Dynamics 365, Autoline, Excel

Outcome

Vehicle margins and inventory values that management and auditors can rely on.

Automotive · Incentives

Sales Incentive Calculations: New and Pre-Owned Vehicles

Problem

Sales staff incentives are a significant cost and a key motivator. They have to be calculated accurately every month, in line with the approved scheme, for both new and pre-owned vehicle sales.

My Approach

  • Prepare the monthly sales staff incentive calculations for new and pre-owned vehicles
  • Calculate payouts in line with the approved incentive schemes
  • Incentive cost reflected in margin analysis and management reporting

Tools

Excel, Microsoft Dynamics 365 sales data

Outcome

Accurate, timely monthly incentive payouts for the sales teams.

Close · IFRS · Team

Month-End Close and IFRS Reporting with a Team of 5

Problem

Multiple automotive entities need a fast, controlled close that produces IFRS financial statements and audit-ready schedules.

My Approach

  • Lead a finance team of 5, setting priorities, deadlines and standards for the close and reporting cycle, and reviewing their work
  • Own month-end and year-end close on Microsoft Dynamics 365
  • Prepare IFRS financial statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) and audit-ready schedules
  • Inter-company, bank, customer, supplier and balance sheet reconciliations, including multi-currency balances

Tools

Microsoft Dynamics 365, Excel with Power Query

Outcome

Books closed in 4 working days, with IFRS statements and audit-ready schedules for each entity.
